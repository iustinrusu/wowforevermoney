# Review, 2026-11-13: UI/UX, money-making strategies, always-correct data, item database

Written as a WoW: Forever player (level 42 rogue, Skinning 270, Fishing 232, Cooking/First Aid 225,
Leatherworking 161, Poisons 210, very little gold). It covers what the app gets right, what to change, and what
was fixed today.

## 1. Item database: fixed today (the priority)

**What was wrong.** The client's own item table (wago.tools export) ships only 19,326 of 32,008 items. The
other 12,682 were known only by name (Questie) or from slow Wowhead tooltip lookups (3,147 priced items).
Worse, the server had *changed* 1,523 shipped items (prices and other fields), so some of our "exact" data was
out of date.

**Fix: the server's own data.** Forever streams item records from the server, and your client keeps them in
`Cache/ADB/enUS/DBCache.bin`. The app now decodes it (`wfm/hotfix.py`):
- **5,238 items added and 1,523 updated** with exact Forever data. All **7,304 priced items** are now fully
  covered by Forever game data (0 Wowhead fallbacks).
- The record layout isn't in the file, so it's inferred from items present in both places, and every field is
  graded. 96.9% of overlapping items decode field-for-field to the client table; the rest differ only where
  the server changed them.
- Four fields fail the grading and are never used: LanguageID, LockID, PageID, RequiredHoliday. In the binary,
  PageID comes before LockID, the reverse of the table's order.
- Re-checked on every start (0.7 s) and re-inferred automatically after a game patch.

**Fix: your game client as the referee.** Addon 1.4.0 asks your client about a list of items the app writes
(`Checks.lua`): sell price, quality, item level, required level, stack size, binding. Its answers override every
other source, and each is scored against the source it replaced, so the app learns which sources are
trustworthy. The list is chosen in priority order:
1. **Disagreements:** 303 items where server data and Wowhead differ on sell price.
2. **Items behind your current recommendations.**
3. **Items only Wowhead or Questie know.**
4. **A random 100 that rotates daily**, so errors anywhere eventually surface.

Results show on Data health ("Item data checked in your game").

**Still open:** 6,460 items have no full record anywhere local. Almost none are traded (every priced item is
covered). Locked containers can't be flagged from the server data (LockID is untrusted).

**Source priority now:** your game client > server data (hotfix cache) > client tables > Wowhead tooltip >
Questie name.

**Proposed next (not built):**
- **Price trend from our own history:** flag items whose lowest buyout fell or rose fast over the last scans,
  and use a short median instead of a single scan for items with few listings.
- **Sale-rate aware demand:** an item listed 3x at 0.3 sale rate needs ~3 listing cycles. Count the AH deposit
  per relist once Forever's deposit rate is confirmed.
- **Alerts when a top pick's confidence drops** (for example, its price looks off after a scan).

## 3. UI/UX as a player

**What works:** detected character and gold up top; one place to start (Top picks); every number explains its
source; flags with hover text; the two fishing columns; Data health as an honesty page.

**Changed today:**
- Picks ranked by profit x confidence (flags lower the rank).
- Snipes you can't afford are flagged and listed after the ones you can.
- "Unlock by levelling" / "Worth switching" are no longer emptied by affordability, and say why when empty.
- Fishing rows say "Per catch".
- A stale-price banner appears when TSM data is old.

**Proposed (bigger, for your OK):**
1. **A plan, not just lists.** A "Today" card: "You have 3s. 1) Fish Feralas for an hour (~3.8g, more with
   pools). 2) Open your trunks. 3) Sell X, Y; keep Z (price rising). 4) With ~4g, start crafting Cloudy
   Gustwoven Boots." It's built from the same data, but tells you what to do in order, with capital snowballing.
2. **Gold per hour for everything.** "Per day" assumes the market absorbs N units a day. As a player, the
   question is "what's the best use of my next hour?". Vendor flips and crafts need travel and AH time; give
   each a time estimate and rank by gold/hour too.
3. **Fewer, clearer flags.** Show one confidence badge (high/medium/low) with the detailed flags on hover.
   Today a row can carry four or five orange labels.
4. **Sell-or-keep advisor in My bags,** using price history: "sell now", "hold (price recovering)", "open it",
   "vendor it".
5. **Search everywhere:** a search box in the header that jumps to any item.
6. **Phone-friendly layout,** so it can sit on a second screen or phone while you play.

## 4. Money-making strategies: brainstorm, and how the current picks hold up

**How today's picks hold up**
- **"Do these now" is capital-starved, not wrong.** With 3s, every row is 1-16 units/day, so the per-day
  numbers are small. The best move right now is a zero-capital activity that turns time into gold (fishing,
  skinning, mob farming), *then* flips and crafts. The planned "Today" card fixes that ordering.
- **Vendor flips:** Junglevine Wine, recipes and Flash Powder look legitimate, but several carry "sells for less"
  (sale rate under 0.5), so buy 1-2, not a stack. Event vendors (Darkmoon Faire, holidays) are now kept out.
- **Fishing (open water):** about 3-4 g/h in Feralas, Tanaris and Azshara; much more with pools (Stranglethorn
  goes from 1.4 to 5.4 g/h). Fine for Fishing/Cooking skill-ups and calm play, but probably *not* your best
  gold/hour.
- **Snipes (AH → vendor):** real but one-off. TSM's Sniper in game catches them better; keep as a watchlist.

**Strategies the app doesn't cover yet (ranked by likely value for you)**
1. **Skinning 270 (Rugged Leather ~12-14s and Thick Leather ~5s each, ~600-1,000 sold/day region-wide).**
   Probably your best gold/hour: high-level beasts (Un'Goro, Felwood, Winterspring, Western Plaguelands) give
   Rugged Leather. Needs skinning-spot data: the addon already logs your skinning loot and time, and Wowhead
   Forever NPC pages have skinning tabs. **Recommended next build.**
2. **Rogue pickpocketing + lockboxes.** Pickpocketing humanoids gives coins and junkboxes (lockboxes) on top of
   their normal loot; your Lockpicking opens them. A classic rogue money-maker, and it combines with cloth
   farming (Mageweave/Runecloth bolts sell 26-34s each). Needs pickpocket data per mob (Wowhead Forever, or the
   addon logging your pickpockets).
3. **Mob farming for cloth and BoE greens.** The addon already measures gold/hour per zone for "mobs"; it needs
   play time to show results.
4. **Crafting for levelling demand.** Players levelling professions buy mats (Light/Medium Leather, Linen/Wool)
   and cheap trainable crafts. The app has sold/day; a "who buys this" view would help.
5. **Disenchanting with your enchanter alt.** BoE greens from trunks and mobs often sell for less than their
   dust/essence. Needs Forever disenchant tables (Wowhead "disenchanting" tabs) and your alt's skill.
6. **Pool route fishing.** Stranglethorn pools are worth ~5.4 g/h before search time. Once the addon measures
   your pool share and time between pools, the app can say whether a pool route beats open water.
7. **Event timing.** The Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza (Classic: Sundays; Tastyfish and rare fish), the
   Darkmoon Faire (turn-ins, event vendors) and seasonal fish (Winter Squid now). This needs Forever's event
   calendar.
8. **Black Market agents (Forever, e.g. Riverglades).** Special stock at their own prices; the addon records
   what they sell.
9. **Cooking buff food for raiders and levellers.** It's in the crafting list. Check sale rates; many are
   flagged "sells for less".
