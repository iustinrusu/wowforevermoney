## 2b. "Worth if crafted" only for what can really be made and sold (C7: Claude's recommendation)

Today, where the app values an ingredient by its best use, every unit counts at that crafted worth when one
recipe turns it into something dear (step 1 checks exactly where). That holds for the first one, not for a stack. Example: Thorium
Bar sells for 17s but shows 21g 65s "if crafted" through Transmute: Arcanite, which can be done once every 2 days
and whose market takes about 0.5 a day at your share. So a farm or a bag of 20 bars looks worth 433g instead of
about 25g.

The rule: crafted worth counts only for as many units a day as the recipe can use (its cooldown, the product's
market at your share: `units_per_day` and `doable_units_per_day` from `best_uses`, times the ingredient's count per
craft); every unit beyond that is worth its sale price (AH or vendor, the usual rules).

1. Find every place that uses crafted worth (`best_uses`, `sell_value`, `doable_value`, valuation of farmed and
   owned items, purse/bag values, Investments) and apply the cap there. For an hourly row (farming, fishing,
   gathering), the units an hour beyond the daily cap are valued at the sale price.
2. Say it plainly in the UI: "worth X crafted, for up to N a day (the recipe's cooldown / what its market takes);
   the rest at the sale price Y".
3. Tests worked out by hand: the Thorium Bar case (0.5 a day crafted, the rest at 17s), a recipe without a
   cooldown whose market takes 40 a day (no change for a few units), an ingredient used 3 per craft.
4. `anomalies.check_crafted_worth` should then mostly go quiet: keep it as a monitor, and check that its findings
   (8 ingredients on 2026-11-14) are now valued sensibly.
