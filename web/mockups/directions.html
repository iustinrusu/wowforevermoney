Three looks to pick from

Each box below is a small copy of the app with made-up numbers: the header, the plan panel and a table. Scroll inside a box to see the parallax (the header's layers move at different speeds), hover the rows, cards and tabs, click a tab, and tick a plan step. In all three the numbers, gold amounts, warnings and hover help keep today's size and contrast: the effects sit around the data, never on top of it. Mixing is fine, for example "A's header with B's panels".

Motion (the new Settings switch):

A · Night watch atmospheric, the most "game-like"

A night sky over two mountain ranges, a faint rune circle turning behind the name, embers rising, and a gold trim that a light runs along now and then. Panels are dark stone with a thin gold frame and corner pieces; titles get a gold rule. Rare and epic item names get a faint glow in their colour.

  • Motion: parallax header, tab underline that slides, rows and cards rise in, medal shine, sparkle when you tick a step.
  • Readability: the table area stays plain dark; rare blue and epic purple a touch lighter so they read better on dark (today they are below the usual contrast for small text).

B · Leather and ledger warm and crafted, the calmest

A tooled leather band with stitching and brass rivets, an old map's contour lines and a compass behind it, lit by a slow candle-like glow. Panels are dark leather with a stitched edge and a wax seal by the title; tables read like a ledger (small-caps headings, a double rule, dotted lines). Alerts arrive as a parchment note with a seal, dark text on light for reading at a glance.

  • Motion: less than A (the map drifts as you scroll, the compass turns, the glow breathes); the same entrances, tab slide and tick sparkle.
  • Readability: warm off-white text on near-black brown, the same contrast as today; the parchment note is the highest-contrast element on the page.

C · Arcane glass modern and the flashiest

A night sky with slow aurora in violet, teal and gold and three layers of stars at different depths. Panels are smooth glass-like cards; table rows are separate rounded strips; the number-one pick has a light running round its border. Gold amounts use coin icons as in game instead of the letters g, s, c.

  • Motion: the most (aurora drifts all the time in the header, the running border, glows on hover); the same entrances, tab slide and tick sparkle.
  • Readability: data on solid dark strips (no see-through behind numbers); the coin icons are optional: the letters can stay.
Made for this app in CSS and SVG: no Blizzard art, fonts or textures. Headings use Cinzel, a free font (SIL Open Font License) the app would ship with; without it they fall back to Palatino as today. Item icons stay as they are. Every effect switches off with the Motion switch, and by default whenever your system asks for less motion.
